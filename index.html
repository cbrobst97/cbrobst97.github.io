Caleb Brobst

Predoctoral Research Fellow, Stanford University

I am a predoctoral research fellow at Stanford University, working with Professor Neale Mahoney. My work there spans gasoline price pass-through and forecasting, retail station entry and exit as electric vehicles spread, and equilibrium models of early childhood care and education policy.

My research interests are in industrial organization, energy and environmental economics, and political economy. I received a B.S. in Economics from Carnegie Mellon University in 2025, where I wrote my honors thesis with Professor Nicholas Muller. As an undergraduate, I also interned at the White House Council of Economic Advisers and the Office of Management and Budget.
